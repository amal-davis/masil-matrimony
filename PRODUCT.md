# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Malayali families and singles, primarily in Kerala, with a meaningful share elsewhere in India and abroad. Two overlapping groups:

- **Parents and relatives (often 50+)** searching on behalf of a son, daughter or sibling. Frequently on mid-range Android phones, sometimes with limited English comfort, and cautious about sharing personal details online. They are evaluating whether this service can be trusted with their family's reputation.
- **Singles themselves** (mid-20s to 30s), searching independently or alongside family, comparing services before registering.

Audience spans communities and religions (Hindu, Christian, Muslim and inter-community), so community-based search and filtering are expected, not optional.

The job to be done: find a suitable, genuine match within the right community, safely, with as much or as little personal help as the family wants.

## Product Purpose

A professional matrimonial service with two equal paths to conversion:

1. **Self-serve:** register a free profile, search and connect with verified matches.
2. **Assisted:** leave an enquiry or request a callback; a relationship advisor (telecaller) follows up personally, leading to assisted/premium plans.

The public website must earn trust quickly and make both paths obvious from the first screen. Success looks like steady free registrations, a healthy flow of qualified enquiries for the CRM team, and families who feel safe recommending the service to relatives.

The member portal, admin panel and CRM tools are separate product surfaces served by the same platform; they may override this register per task.

## Brand Personality

**Trusted, warm, rooted.**

- Speaks like a respected family matchmaker: courteous, reassuring, plain-spoken. Never pushy, never flirtatious.
- Rooted in Kerala culture and family values without leaning on clichés.
- Confidence comes from substance (verification, privacy controls, real people to talk to), not from hype.
- Emotional goal: a parent closes the page thinking "these people are serious and they will take care of us."

**Reference: [nestmatrimony.com](https://nestmatrimony.com/).** What to take from it:

- Regional Kerala focus and a community-aware vocabulary (religion, caste, district).
- A home page that does work: inline free registration and a quick bride/groom search near the top.
- Trust content: verification, success stories, real offices and staff, an assisted premium service with a personal advisor.
- Browse-by-community and browse-by-place entry points.
- An information-rich page rather than a sparse one.

Take the content model and conversion paths, not the execution. Present this information with clearer hierarchy, less repetition and more care than a typical portal. Trust claims must be real and consistent (no inflated or conflicting numbers).

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS landing pages:** gradient blobs, metric heroes ("5 Lakh+" as a giant stat block), identical icon-card feature grids, startup tone.
- **Wedding-card kitsch:** heavy gold foil, mandala borders, ornate script fonts used everywhere, faux-luxury ornamentation.
- **Dating-app vibe:** swipe mechanics, playful or flirtatious copy, casual neon aesthetics. This is about marriage and families.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust before ask.** Every request for personal information is preceded by a reason to trust: verification, privacy controls, a real person to call. Never demand data the user hasn't been given a reason to share.
2. **Two doors, both open.** Self-serve registration and assisted enquiry are equal first-class paths on every key page; neither is buried.
3. **Readable by a parent on a phone.** Design for a 55-year-old on a mid-range Android phone in daylight first: large legible text, high contrast, big tap targets, simple words. Everything else follows.
4. **Rooted, not decorated.** Kerala and family warmth come through language, photography and typography, not ornament.
5. **Substance over spectacle.** Show real, verifiable proof (process, people, policies) instead of inflated claims and animated counters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.2 AA** minimum across the public site.
- Older users are a primary audience: generous base font size, high-contrast text (no light-gray body copy), touch targets of at least 44×44 px, clear focus states, no information conveyed by color alone.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; motion is never required to understand content.
- Plain, simple English now; plan for **Malayalam** localisation (layouts must tolerate longer strings and Malayalam script).
- Performance is an accessibility concern: pages must stay fast on mid-range Android devices and patchy mobile networks.
- Privacy-sensitive by default: no personal or contact details shown publicly.
